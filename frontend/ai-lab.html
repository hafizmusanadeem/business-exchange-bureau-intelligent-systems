AI Lab

Four checks that replace manual steps: can we keep a match guarantee, which listings need a person to look, does the report checker catch bad sentences, and how well does every part score. All data is synthetic.

Can we promise the matches?

Counts investors whose sector, budget and minimum EBITDA fit this business. It does not predict that anyone will enquire or buy.

Moderation queue

A flag asks a person to look. It is not a finding of fraud. Only the flagged few need review.

Try to sneak a bad sentence into a report

Sentences must cite a fact such as [F1]. Known facts: F1 Healthcare share up 8.7% to 15.3% (6.5 pts), F2 market figures, F3 Retail share down 19.3% to 10.9%, F5 listings forecast.

Evaluation scoreboard

Measured on synthetic data. It shows the pipeline works, not how it performs on BXB data.
